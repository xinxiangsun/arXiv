Abstract: We use the cyclic figure of merit to determine the likelihood of achieving cyclic deconvolution for 2609 canonical pulsars, a population for which this technique has not previously been used. These pulsars have spin periods between 0.1 and 10 seconds and are surveyed across 15 different telescope-observing frequency combinations. Still, it is reliable for MSPs and for canonicals at high observing frequencies. More promising is the prospect of using this technique to recover scintles for canonical pulsars; DSA and FAST are best-suited for this as judged by the number of sources where scintles may be resolved and S/N is sufficient. We suspect there may be more sources that would have large figures of cyclic merit, but insufficient S/N, at very low frequencies.
