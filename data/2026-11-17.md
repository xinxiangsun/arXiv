<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 利用SymBoltz的符号-数值接口与无近似结构，仅通过指定协变耦合即可紧凑地定义模型；对刚性方程组采用高效隐式求解器，并由符号方程自动生成稀疏解析雅可比矩阵。

Result: 以单相互作用模型进行验证，背景演化、物质功率谱与CMB功率谱同修改版CLASS在多数尺度上一致度达0.1%–1%；完整的三组分耦合模型仅约300行代码，新增任意相互作用只需三行。

Conclusion: 该框架使复杂多组分相互作用模型的实现变得简单、稳定且高效，显著优于CLASS/CAMB中所需的复杂改造。

Abstract: We implement a simple and general way of specifying arbitrary interactions between baryons, dark matter and dark energy in the Einstein--Boltzmann code SymBoltz. Due to the symbolic-numeric interface and approximation-free structure of SymBoltz, the model definition is remarkably compact and convenient to modify by specifying only the covariant coupling. The full equations are stiff, but stable and fast to integrate with an efficient implicit solver, particularly with the sparse and analytical ODE Jacobian generated from the symbolic equations. We first validate it using single-interaction models, comparing their background evolution and matter and CMB power spectra with those from modified versions of CLASS, finding $0.1\%$--$1\%$ agreement at most scales. Finally, we show how our framework makes it trivial to implement models with any interactions of any complexity among all three species simultaneously, which requires a more involved implementation in other Einstein--Boltzmann codes like CLASS or CAMB. Result: 分析强烈指示燃料极端贫氢且贫CNO，X≈0.03、Z_CNO≈4×10⁻⁴；对于轨道周期3.5小时（相对较长）的系统而言这一结果出乎意料。作者探讨了能产生此类天体的演化情形，并结合迄今观测到的三次暂现爆发讨论其历史含义。

Conclusion: 此类分析揭示供体成分分布异常宽广，可能有助于解释已知暴源在暴发性质上的显著多样性；对IGR J17511-3057的贫氢贫CNO结果挑战了"长周期即太阳成分供体"的常规假设。

Abstract: Binary systems in which a neutron star accretes material from a low-mass stellar companion arise from evolutionary processes that depend sensitively on the progenitor properties. It is usually assumed that the accreted material has approximately solar composition, unless the orbital period is $\lesssim80$ min, indicating an "ultracompact" system with a hydrogen deficient donor. However, few constraints on the donor composition are available. Here we apply a Bayesian model-comparison framework, BEANSP, to a sample of 21 thermonuclear bursts from the accretion-powered millisecond pulsar, IGR J17511-3057, to constrain the fuel composition as well as the system parameters. Our analysis strongly indicates an extremely hydrogen- and CNO-deficient fuel composition, with $X \approx 0.03$ and $Z_\rm{CNO}\approx 4 \times 10^{-4}$, unexpected for this system with its relatively long 3.5 hr orbital period. We explore the possible circumstances under which such an object can evolve, and discuss the implications for its history also in light of the three transient outbursts that have been observed to date. Such analyses are finding an unexpectedly wide range in donor compositions, that may help to explain the remarkable diversity in the burst properties of the known burst sources
