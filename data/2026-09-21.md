Method: 使用德国天体物理虚拟天文台（GAVO）DaCHS平台，将档案以虚拟天文台（VO）兼容形式呈现，支持专用网页界面及PyVO、TOPCAT、Aladin等VO工具访问；提供搜索与下载已完成波长定标、宇宙线清理和重子速度改正的一维光谱示例。

Result: 释放约15万条高分辨率光谱，描述数据产品的整体性质；概述仪器性能与自运行以来的主要升级；采用1年标准专有期（部分例外）。

Conclusion: 该文为用户提供HERMES档案所需的数据产品信息，并可作为档案使用的参考论文。

Abstract: Context. The HERMES spectrograph is a high-resolution, fibre-fed optical spectrograph of the 1.2m Mercator telescope operated from the Roque de los Muchachos observatory in Spain. HERMES started its scientific operations in June 2009 and has been the workhorse instrument since then. Regular instrument updates have been crucial. They have helped to ensure the continued provision of high-quality spectra. This will continue both now and in the future.
  Aims. With this contribution we review over 16 years of HERMES operations and we launch and introduce the archive to the interna- tional community at large. We describe the overall performances of the HERMES spectrograph and list the most important instrument upgrades since the start of the operations. We release about 150 000 high-resolution spectra to the community and describe global properties of the datarelease. We use a standard propriety period of 1 year with some exceptions.
  Methods. We used the German Astrophysical Virtual Observatory (GAVO) DaCHS platform to present the archive in a Virtual Ob- servatory (VO) compatible form with both an access via a dedicated webinterface, and via VO-compliant tools like PyVO, TOPCAT, Aladin etc. We illustrate the use in several worked-out examples to search and download the fully wavelength-calibrated, cosmic- cleaned and barycentric corrected 1D spectra.
  Results. With this contribution we provide the needed information of the data product and offer a reference paper for the users of the HERMES archive.
